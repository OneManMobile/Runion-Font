Runion Basic is a monospaced rune face: the Elder Futhark, the oldest runic alphabet, rebuilt on a grid of 3 by 7 dots. Every glyph is made of straight lines drawn from dot to dot, the way runes were once cut into wood, bone and stone, and every letter fills the full width of its narrow, tall cell.

Type ordinary text and get runes. The 24 runes keep their historical structure; typing th or ng contracts into the single runes thurisaz and ingwaz, as a rune carver would have written them. Letters the Elder Futhark never had are borrowed from later rune rows, such as the medieval Scandinavian runes for æ and ø, or invented on the same grid, as are the numbers, symbols and accents. Capital letters are the very same runes traced with a heavier line, and the runes of the Unicode Runic block are included too.

The whole typeface is defined in a single text file of dot-to-dot connections. To contribute, see github.com/OneManMobile/Runion-Font.
