Runion Basic is a monospaced rune typeface. It redraws the Elder Futhark, the oldest runic alphabet, on a grid of 3 by 7 dots. The Elder Futhark was in use centuries before the Viking Age. Every glyph is made of straight lines from dot to dot, and every letter fills the full width of its narrow, tall cell.

Type ordinary text and you get runes. The 24 runes keep their historical structure, and typing th or ng gives the single runes thurisaz and ingwaz. Letters the Elder Futhark did not have are borrowed from later rune rows, such as the medieval Scandinavian runes for æ and ø, or drawn new on the same grid. The numbers, symbols and accents are new as well. Capital letters are the same runes drawn with a heavier line. The runes of the Unicode Runic block are included.

The typeface is defined in one text file of dot-to-dot connections. To contribute, see github.com/OneManMobile/Runion-Font.
